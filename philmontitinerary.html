Conservation Project

🌳
North Fork Urraca — New Trail Construction
Ascending out of the bottom of the North Fork Urraca Canyon, this new trail will eventually connect to Stockade Ridge Camp — providing unique views of the Tooth of Time ridge. Crews help build a trail through steep terrain, meadowy benches, and rocky canyons. Be prepared to hike up to a mile from the bottom to the construction site. A required part of your Arrowhead Award — your 3-hour contribution matters!

Logistics & Key Notes

🚌 TransportationBus provided from Camping HQ to Zastrow Trailhead on Day 2. Crew hikes back to Camping HQ on Day 9 via the Tooth of Time trail.
🐄 Horse RideScheduled during Trip Planning in Logistics on Day 1. Horse rides depart from Beaubien. Strict height/weight requirements apply — see Horse Ride chart. Not available July 4th.
🌶 Food PickupsDay 2: Camping HQ (initial issue). Day 5: Phillips Junction (passthrough resupply from Beaubien area). Plan your meals to bridge between pickups.
💧 Water SourcesDay 8 is a dry camp at Shaefers Pass. Water available at North Fork Urraca Creek. Fill up before reaching camp. All other camps have water access.
🏡 Camp Types4 Staffed (ABREU, CRATER LAKE, BEAUBIEN, BLACK MOUNTAIN) — showers available at Beaubien. 3 Trail Camps (Porcupine, Red Hills + Shaefers Pass as dry camp).
🍻 FishingSelf-guided fishing opportunities may be available on this itinerary. A valid New Mexico Fishing License is required. Bring personal gear.
🏅 Awards EligibleAll crews earn the Arrowhead Award upon completing the conservation project. 46 miles qualifies toward the 50-Miler Award if combined with home-area conservation hours.
⛰ Mileage Note46 miles is camp-to-camp only. Expect 25–30% more actual hiking including side hikes to peaks, conservation sites, and program areas. Daily average ~5.75 mi.
