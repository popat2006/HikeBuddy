# HikeBuddy

## Purpose
A comprehensive resource for hikers of all levels, with a special focus on helping newcomers discover the joy of hiking.

## Website
Visit our GitHub Pages website: **[HikeBuddy Website](https://popat2006.github.io/HikeBuddy/)**

The website features multiple tabs with helpful information:
- **Home** - Welcome and introduction
- **Getting Started** - Essentials for beginner hikers
- **Gear Guide** - What to bring and gear recommendations
- **Safety Tips** - Weather, wildlife, and emergency preparedness
- **Trail Finder** - Trail listings and recommendations (content coming soon)
- **Hiking 101** - Techniques, terminology, and trail etiquette
- **Photo Gallery** - Hiking photos showcase (content coming soon)
- **Resources** - External links, apps, and helpful websites
- **FAQ** - Common questions from new hikers

## What You'll Find Here
- Trail mapping and GPS tracking tools
- Hiking gear checklists and equipment logs
- Weather and safety information resources
- Personal hiking journals and trip reports
- Photo organization and gallery systems
- Route planning and navigation aids
- Fitness tracking and workout logs related to hiking
- Community sharing and collaboration features for hikers

## Usage
Feel free to add any hiking-related projects, scripts, documents, or resources that enhance your outdoor adventures. This serves as a central hub for all your hiking technology and organization needs.
